Our Methodology

A Structured, Professional Workflow

Clear communication, transparent milestones, and structured revision rounds ensure seamless collaboration on every project.

Step 01

Discovery & Briefing

We define your project objectives, target audience, core deliverables, and stylistic references to establish a clear creative brief.

Step 02

Planning & Assembly

Raw media is organized, synced, and screened. We build the initial assembly cut, establishing narrative structure and foundational pacing.

Step 03

Editing & Refinement

We layer sound design, dynamic transitions, color correction passes, and motion graphics, incorporating client feedback through structured review rounds.

Step 04

Final Delivery

Final assets are rendered in uncompressed broadcast specifications and platform-optimized codecs for web, social media, or broadcast distribution.

About Mercy

The Creative Behind the Edit

I am Mercy Olufunke Adekunle, a Senior Video Editor based in Abuja, Nigeria, working with international brands, commercial studios, and fast-growing creative agencies.

My academic background in Performing Arts from the University of Ilorin gives me a unique advantage: I approach video editing through dramatic arc, performance nuance, and rhythmic timing rather than mere mechanical cuts.

Over the past 5+ years, I have spearheaded post-production pipelines spanning CapCut-driven viral workflows, Adobe Premiere Pro commercial campaigns, and DaVinci Resolve color finishing.
